Hello, I'm Isaque Accarini!


I am an Artificial Intelligence student at UNIMAR (University of Marília) and a dedicated tech enthusiast. My connection to computing and the internet goes back to when I was just four years old, making technology a natural part of my life. During high school at Colégio Interação, my passion for the field grew, and by 2024 I had set my mind on joining the AI program at UNIMAR, drawn by its inspiring campus and environment focused on discovery and personal growth. Choosing this path became even more special when my lifelong friends decided to join the course alongside me, including one who enrolled a bit later in the semester.

This website was built as my Homepage Project for Harvard's CS50 course. Rather than doing the bare minimum, I wanted to build something well-structured and meaningful. This project introduced me to Bootstrap, which I learned to leverage alongside HTML and CSS to create a clean, responsive layout. The site functions as my personal digital hub with three primary sections: Vision, Curriculum Vitae, and Portfolio.
